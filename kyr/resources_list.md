# Know Your Rights (KYR) Resources for the Undocumented Community
- **CASA-Basic KYR Resources** Information and sketches about immigrants rights, information about how to/how not to interact with ICE,examples of what a judicial warrant looks like, downloadable guide. [Link](https://wearecasa.org/know-your-rights/)
- **Immigrant Legal Resource Center** [Know Your Rights Toolkit](https://www.ilrc.org/community-resources/know-your-rights)
    - [Red Cards](https://www.ilrc.org/redcards) in 16 languages.
    - [Family Preparedness Plan](https://www.ilrc.org/community-resources/know-your-rights/step-step-family-preparedness-plan).
    - KYR [tutorial videos](https://www.wehaverights.us/).
    - California Rapid Response Networks [phone numbers](https://www.ccijustice.org/carrn).
    - Non-profit legal services [directory](https://www.immigrationadvocates.org/nonprofit/legaldirectory/).
- **United We Dream** [KYR](https://unitedwedream.org/resources/know-your-rights/) in different scenarios, avaliable in [Spanish]( https://unitedwedream.org/resources/conoce-tus-derechos-una-guia-para-inmigrantes/) as well.
- **SEIU-iAmerica** [KYR](https://iamerica.org/know-your-rights/), red Cards, what to do if ICE questions you. 
- **Crowdsource Materials** Community [compilation](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1QJ_pDXZ0_nfcxIKKA5GbdOYr53uuyZQ1HDVYxWoEHS0/htmlview?gid=0#gid=0) of resources labeled by applicable state and language.
