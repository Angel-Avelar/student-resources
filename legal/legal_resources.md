# Legal Resources for the Undocumented Community
- **National Immigration Law Center** Fights for immigrant access to healthcare, education and fair work conditions
- **CHIR-LA** California-based, immigrant rights, civil rights, and human rights organization with national impact
- **Asian Americans Advancing Justice** The largest legal and civil rights organization for API communities
- **FWD.US, Los Angeles Chapter** Organization that mobilizes tech community to support immigrant rights
